Electric bike servicing

We service electric bicycles with Bosch, Shimano STEPS and Yamaha mid-drive motors, and most hub-motor conversions. Our electric bike service costs 129 and includes a battery health report, motor diagnostics and a firmware update where the manufacturer provides one.

What we cannot do

We cannot open sealed battery packs or repair motors. If a diagnostic shows a failed battery or motor, we will give you a written report that you can use for a manufacturer warranty claim.

Before you bring your bike in

  • Bring the charger and both keys, if your battery has a lock.
  • Charge the battery to at least half before dropping off.
  • Tell us about any error codes you have seen.
