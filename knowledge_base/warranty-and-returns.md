# Warranty and returns

## Workmanship guarantee

All repair work is guaranteed for 90 days from the collection date. If the same fault returns
within 90 days because of our workmanship, we fix it free of charge. The guarantee does not cover
normal wear, damage from crashes, or parts that you supplied yourself.

To use the guarantee, bring the bike and your receipt to the workshop. You do not need a new
booking, but calling ahead helps us plan.

## Parts warranty

Parts we supply carry the manufacturer's warranty. Tyres, inner tubes, brake pads, chains and
cables are consumable items and are not covered for wear.

## Returns

Unused parts in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days with a receipt, for a
refund to the original payment method. Parts that have been fitted cannot be returned unless they
are faulty. Special-order parts cannot be returned.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with a repair, email help@spokeandchain.example or speak to the workshop
manager. We reply to written complaints within 3 working days.
