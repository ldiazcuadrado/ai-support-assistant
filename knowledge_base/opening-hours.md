# Opening hours

## Regular hours

| Day              | Hours         |
| ---------------- | ------------- |
| Monday to Friday | 08:00 - 18:00 |
| Saturday         | 09:00 - 16:00 |
| Sunday           | Closed        |

Bikes can be dropped off from opening time until 30 minutes before closing. Collections are
possible until closing time.

## Public holidays

We are closed on public holidays. In December we close at 14:00 on 24 December and reopen on
2 January.

## Out-of-hours drop-off

A locked drop box for keys is next to the front door. Leave your bike in the secure rack in the
side yard, put your name, phone number and a note describing the problem in the key envelope, and
we will call you the next working day.
