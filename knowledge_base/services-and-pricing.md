# Services and pricing

All prices include labour and VAT. Parts are charged separately unless stated otherwise. We
always confirm the final price with you before starting work that goes beyond the quoted service.

## Service packages

| Service               | Price | What is included                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Safety check          | 25    | Brakes, tyres, gears, wheels and bolts inspected; minor adjustments                            |
| Standard service      | 79    | Safety check plus gear and brake adjustment, drivetrain clean, lubrication                     |
| Full service          | 149   | Standard service plus wheel truing, bearing check and re-grease, new cables and housing fitted |
| Electric bike service | 129   | Standard service plus battery, motor and firmware diagnostics                                  |

## Common repairs

- Puncture repair (tube patched or replaced, one wheel): 15 plus the cost of a new tube if needed
- Tyre replacement, fitting only: 12 per tyre
- Brake pad replacement: 18 per wheel, plus pads
- Chain replacement: 20, plus the chain
- Gear cable replacement: 22 per cable
- Wheel truing: 25 per wheel
- Hydraulic brake bleed: 30 per brake

## Parts

Parts are sold at the price on the shelf label. If we have to order a part, we ask for a 50%
deposit on parts costing more than 100.

We do not offer frame repair, carbon fibre repair or painting.
