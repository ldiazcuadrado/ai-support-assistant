# About Spoke & Chain Cycle Works

Spoke & Chain Cycle Works is a fictional bicycle repair shop. It exists only as sample data for
the ai-support-assistant project; every name, price and policy in this knowledge base is invented.

We repair and maintain road, mountain, commuter, cargo and electric bicycles. We do not sell new
bicycles. We do sell a small range of parts and accessories, such as tyres, inner tubes, chains,
brake pads, lights and locks.

## Where to find us

The workshop is at 14 Foundry Lane, Marlowe Bridge. It is a five minute walk from Marlowe Bridge
railway station. There are bike racks outside and a free customer car park behind the building.
