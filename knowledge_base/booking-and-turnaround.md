# Booking and turnaround

## Booking

You can book a service online at spokeandchain.example/book, by phone or by walking in. Walk-ins
are welcome for puncture repairs and safety checks. Standard, full and electric bike services
should be booked, because we limit the number of bikes we take in each day.

You can cancel or move a booking free of charge up to 24 hours before the drop-off time. After
that, a 10 fee applies for a missed booking.

## How long will it take?

| Service               | Typical turnaround              |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Puncture repair       | Same day, usually within 1 hour |
| Safety check          | Same day                        |
| Standard service      | 2 working days                  |
| Full service          | 3 working days                  |
| Electric bike service | 3 working days                  |

Turnaround times are estimates. In spring (March to May) workshops are busy and a standard service
can take up to 5 working days. If we have to order parts we will tell you the expected delay when
we call with the quote.

## Collection

We call or text you when your bike is ready. Bikes can be stored free for 7 days after that.
After 7 days we charge 2 per day for storage. Bikes not collected within 60 days of the ready
notice may be sold to cover costs.

## Courtesy bikes

We do not currently lend courtesy bikes.
