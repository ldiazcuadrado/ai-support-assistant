Frequently asked questions

Do you repair children's bikes?
Yes. Children's bikes are serviced at the same prices as adult bikes, except the safety check, which is free for bikes with wheels of 20 inches or smaller.

Can I wait while you fix a puncture?
Yes. Puncture repairs are done while you wait, usually within an hour. There is a seating area and free coffee.

Do you collect and deliver bikes?
No. We do not offer a collection or delivery service at the moment. Customers need to bring bikes to the workshop.

Do you accept card payments?
We accept all major credit and debit cards and cash. We do not accept cheques or cryptocurrency.

Can I leave my bike overnight if I cannot collect it the same day?
Yes. Storage is free for 7 days after we tell you the repair is finished.

Do you work on bikes bought elsewhere or online?
Yes, we work on any brand. We may refuse bikes that are unsafe to ride, such as frames with cracks.

Do you offer discounts?
Members of the Marlowe Cycling Club receive 10% off labour when they show their membership card. This discount cannot be combined with other offers.
